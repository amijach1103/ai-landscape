# Example entry: the hedge is the finding

"Suggests" and "shows" are not stylistic variants. When a summary upgrades one
to the other, the claim changes and nothing in the output marks it. The reader
of the summary has no way to recover what was actually said.

## Why it matters here

`aijournal/faithfulness.py` treats a dropped hedge as a failure of the same
class as an invented number, because both leave you holding a claim the source
did not make.
